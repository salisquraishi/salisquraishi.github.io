My name is Salis. An engineering graduate and a computer professional who spent almost two decades in technology industry as of Oct 1, 2026. The day I started this blog.   

I was born and brought up in Indian state Chhattisgarh. My native place is Balod, a small sleepy town approximately 100 KM south west of Chhattisgarh's capital Raipur. 

I spent my two decades of professional life on tier one cities of India like, Pune, Chennai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru etc, working in tech companies but I am still so much homesick that even the thought of not being able to visit my beautify town Balod, can make me depressed. This is the place I was born, this is the place I would like to breath my last.

## Why to write when AI writes better

This is the irony with me. I suddenly fell in love with bicycle and have been cycling since then and that fascination with a bicycle made me an avid tourer. I have travelled thousands of miles on my cycle through dense forrest, remote villages, even inside wild life sanctuaries and inside hardcore maoists zone of Chhattisgarh. I still do not know how I fell in love with bicycle and why I keep riding but I do know -- its a pure joy. 

My infatuation with Guitar and love for playing flute, reading newspapers or romance with making biryani has similar emotions involved and I don't want to ponder over the reasons because it all gave me immense joy, pleasure and satisfaction. 

I think the same applies to this blog. I am writing because I want to write; not for fame, not of money and not even for attention, just for the sake of writing, just for the joy of writing.. But I am aware of the fact that I have started writing when reading habit is dwindling faster than the morality and ethics.
