Hatasha se ek vyakti baith gaya tha

I heard about Vinod Kumar Shukla when he died and twitter flooded my timeline with his poetry, snippets of poetries and quotes from his novels and stories. I thought to know more about him and searched some details, I was shocked to learned that he was from Rajnandgao. He was honored with top Indian literary award - Sahitya Akademy Award in the year 1999. The town 50 KM from my native. I got interested in his writings and read some pieces. I was completely blown away by his simple yet profound style of expressing complex thoughts.

His few line kept on humming in my mind on the day of his demise.

Hatasha se ek vyakti baith gaya tha
Vyakti ko main nahi janta tha
Hatasha ko janta tha

Isliye main us vyakti ke pas gaya
Maine hath barhaya
Mera hath pakad kar wo khada hua

Mujhe wo nahi janta tha
Mere hath barhane ko janta tha

Hum dono sath chale
Dono ek doosare ko nahi jante the
Sath chalne ko jante the
